What does a count include?

A store user and a central user both see a "device count." Should those numbers mean the same thing? I asked which locations, device types and filters each role's total should include, checked the summary against the records behind it, and brought the product owner one precise question to decide.

BeforeSame word, two different totals. Nobody sure which is right.

A case from my job, told with illustrative data
Public project · Transit data

Who the turnstiles miss.

The MBTA publishes two official ridership datasets that disagree by up to 23% on the Blue Line. Someone asked why on the MBTA's developer forum, and the gap was blamed on growth in East Boston.

I tested that with a third, independent source. Fare taps, the trip model and a 2025 rider survey all point the same way: the gap comes from riders who change lines underground at four downtown hubs, where no fare gate sees them.

  1. 01Reproduced the forum's numbers exactly across 6 fall seasons (2017–2025), then extended the test to all 61 gated subway stations.
  2. 02Validated with the 2025 rider survey: taps plus survey-reported transfers land within 3% of the model at the four downtown hubs.
  3. 03Logged 16 data quality issues and wrote 5 recommendations for the data owners.
