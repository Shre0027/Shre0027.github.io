04 · Public project · Transit data
Data reconciliationPython · pandasGeospatial · GTFSData quality log

Who the turnstiles miss

The MBTA publishes two official ridership datasets that disagree by up to 23% on the Blue Line. I reconciled them with a third, independent source and an animated map of the whole subway.

BeforeA public question on the MBTA's developer forum, with the gap blamed on growth in East Boston.
AfterThree datasets (fare taps, the trip model and a 2025 rider survey) agree: the gap comes from riders who change lines underground at four downtown hubs, where no fare gate sees them.
  • Reconciled official MBTA data across 6 fall seasons (2017–2025), reproducing the forum's figures exactly, then extended the test to all 61 gated subway stations.
  • Validated with the 2025 rider survey: taps plus survey-reported transfers land within 3% of the model at the four downtown hubs.
  • Built an exact bridge between the two numbers, stress-tested it against bus data and the MBTA's own shutdown alerts, logged 16 data quality issues (including a split that flipped overnight on March 6, 2019), and wrote 5 recommendations for the data owners.
